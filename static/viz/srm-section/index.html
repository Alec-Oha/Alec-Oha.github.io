STANDBY
Governing equations
Burn rate
r = a Pnc

Saint-Robert's law. The burning surface recedes faster as chamber pressure rises.

Chamber pressure
Pc = (a ρp Ab c*At)1/(1−n)

Gas generated at the burning surface balances gas choked through the throat. As the bore grows, Ab and Pc rise.

Area–Mach relation
AA* = 1M[2γ + 1(1 + γ − 12 M2)]γ + 12(γ − 1)

Local flow area sets the Mach number. The flow is subsonic through the approach, sonic at the throat and supersonic in the exit cone.

Static temperature
TT0 = [1 + γ − 12 M2]−1

Gas cools as it accelerates, which is what the particle color shows.

Thrust
F = ṁ ve + (pe − pa) Ae

Momentum thrust from the exhaust, plus a pressure term at the exit plane.

Geometry illustrative. Gas velocities upstream of the nozzle are exaggerated for visibility.
